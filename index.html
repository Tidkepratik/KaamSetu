Government support when you hire apprentices

~25%
NAPS — stipend reimbursement

National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme reimburses part of the prescribed stipend for trade apprentices (cap applies).

~50%
NATS — for diploma & graduate apprentices

Government reimburses about half of the prescribed minimum stipend to establishments under NATS 2.0.
